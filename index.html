Errors & Lessons
01

Chasing art before planning the blockout

✕ The mistake

Early on I focused on making things look good before figuring out what the level actually needed to do. I'd spend hours on art and atmosphere, then hit a wall — no clear structure, no flow.

✓ The fix

Now I never open the engine without a blockout on paper first. Sketch the layout by hand — rooms, paths, pacing beats — and only then bring it into the engine as a grey box.

02

Designing without playtesting

✕ The mistake

I spent weeks building a combat encounter that felt great in my head — but the first time a fresh player tried it, they got lost immediately.

✓ The fix

I now schedule playtests at every stage. If someone who hasn't seen the level can't figure out where to go, the level failed — not the player.

03

Over-engineering systems early

✕ The mistake

On an early Unity project I built a complex AI behavior system before the core loop even worked. When the design changed, everything broke.

✓ The fix

Prototype the feel first, build the systems after. A bad mechanic with good code is still a bad mechanic.
